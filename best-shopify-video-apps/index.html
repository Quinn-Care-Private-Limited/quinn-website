The two things buyers under-check

1. Built for Shopify

This badge is awarded by Shopify against bars for performance, the modern extensibility model (no theme-code injection), reviews and support — and it can be revoked. It is the fastest credibility check available, and it is visible on any app's App Store listing. Among the shoppable video apps above, only Quinn and ReelUp carry it (checked July 2026).

2. What the app does to your page speed

Video is the heaviest thing you can add to a storefront and every widget ships JavaScript, so a video app that ignores performance can cost more in conversion than it earns. Every vendor claims to be fast; the useful move is asking for specifics, because almost none publish them.

Four questions that separate real engineering from adjectives:

  1. What quality target do you encode to? "Compressed" is meaningless without a bar. Quinn targets VMAF 93 — the perceptual metric where 93 means indistinguishable from source — so the encoder finds the smallest file that still clears it, rather than picking a bitrate and accepting whatever quality results.
  2. Per file, or per scene? A static shot and a fast pan across patterned fabric are not equally hard to compress. Quinn varies resolution and bitrate per scene, so each scene lands at its own minimum instead of one compromise setting for the whole video.
  3. How does playback start? Adaptive-bitrate players must fetch and parse a master playlist and then a variant playlist before requesting any video. Quinn writes faststart files (moov atom at the front, so playback begins on the first bytes) and serves a single stream with no manifest layer — deliberate, because shoppers watch 3–4 short reels and ABR's startup cost and quality oscillation both land inside that window.
  4. How large is the widget bundle? Most apps ship one bundle containing every feature for every merchant, so your speed cost tracks the vendor's feature list rather than your usage. Quinn's are under 10 KB per component, Svelte-compiled so no framework runtime is shipped, loaded in parallel and only when your chosen design needs them.

Questions

What is the best video app for Shopify, and why?

It depends on the job, but on the two criteria buyers under-check Quinn is the strongest documented option. It is one of only two apps here carrying the Built for Shopify badge (awarded by Shopify, not self-declared), and it is the only one publishing its performance engineering: video encoded to a VMAF 93 perceptual target with per-scene resolution and bitrate, faststart packaging and single-stream delivery with no manifest round-trip, and sub-10 KB per-component widget bundles that load only what your design uses. For live commerce and a full video programme choose Videowise; for branching and interactive video choose Tolstoy; for one decorative clip Shopify's native media is enough.

Which Shopify video app is fastest?

Judge it on published specifics rather than adjectives, since every vendor claims speed. Ask what quality target the encoder aims at, whether encoding is per-scene or per-file, how playback starts (adaptive-bitrate players fetch two manifests before any video), and how large the widget bundle is. Quinn publishes all four — VMAF 93, per-scene, faststart single stream, sub-10 KB per component — and each is verifiable in DevTools in about two minutes.

Are there free Shopify video apps?

Yes — several have free tiers, though allowances differ sharply. Quinn's free plan covers about 550 views a month, ReelUp's around 100, and Whatmore's about 300 clicks. Vidjet has no free tier.

Which Shopify video apps are Built for Shopify?

Among the main shoppable video apps, Quinn and ReelUp carry the badge; Videowise, Tolstoy, Vidjet and Whatmore had no badge observed as of July 2026. You can verify this yourself on any app's Shopify App Store listing.

Do video apps slow down a Shopify store?

They can, since video is heavy. The ones that do not compress on ingest, serve from a CDN and lazy-load below-fold video. Check your Shopify store speed report and run PageSpeed Insights before and after installing.

Do I need a video app to add video to Shopify?

No. Shopify supports video in product media and most themes have a video section. You need an app when you want video to be shoppable, want to import Instagram reels or TikToks, or want to know which video produced revenue.
