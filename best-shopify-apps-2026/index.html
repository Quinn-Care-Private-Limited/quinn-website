The best Shopify apps to install in 2026.

There are thousands of Shopify apps, but you don't need thousands — you need the right one for each job. These are the categories that move the needle for almost every store, with one strong, well-reviewed pick each. The theme running through the list: each is Built for Shopify (or first-party), lightweight, and earns its keep.

Curated by category · Built-for-Shopify picks · Free plans where possible

The must-have apps, by category

1.

Klaviyo

Email & SMS

If you add one growth app, make it this. Klaviyo plugs straight into your Shopify data to run segmented email and SMS flows — welcome series, abandoned cart, win-back — that reliably drive a large share of DTC revenue. Free to start, and it scales with your list.

2.

Quinn Shoppable Videos & Reels

Shoppable video

Video is how products get discovered now — but a video only sells if shoppers can buy from it. Quinn is the shoppable-video pick on this list: it turns your product videos, Instagram reels, and TikToks into tappable carousels, stories, and floating widgets right on your store, with add-to-cart and checkout inside the video.

3.

Judge.me

Product reviews

Social proof converts, and Judge.me is one of the most popular ways to collect and display product reviews and star ratings on Shopify — photo reviews, automatic review requests, and rich snippets. It has a generous free plan and stays fast.

4.

Gorgias

Customer support

A helpdesk built for e-commerce: it pulls email, live chat, and social messages into one inbox with full order context, so support is faster and can even drive sales. The common upgrade once a store outgrows a shared Gmail inbox.

5.

Recharge

Subscriptions

If any part of your catalog can sell on repeat, Recharge is the go-to for subscriptions and recurring billing on Shopify — subscribe-and-save, easy subscription management, and retention tools that turn one-time buyers into recurring revenue.

6.

Rebuy

Upsell & cross-sell

Rebuy adds personalized upsells and cross-sells across the journey — product page, cart, and post-purchase — to lift average order value without feeling pushy. One of the most effective AOV levers you can bolt on.

7.

PageFly

Page builder

A no-code, drag-and-drop page builder for landing pages, product pages, and campaigns that go beyond your theme's defaults — no developer required. Flexible, widely used, and free to start.

8.

Smile.io

Loyalty & rewards

Repeat customers are cheaper than new ones. Smile.io adds points, referrals, and VIP tiers to turn first purchases into repeat ones — a proven retention play with an easy setup and a free plan.

9.

Shopify Search & Discovery

Search & filtering

Shopify's own free app upgrades your store's search, filtering, and product recommendations so shoppers actually find what they want. It's native, free, and a quick win for any catalog past a handful of products.

How to think about it

One app per job beats a pile of overlapping ones. Start with wherever your store is weakest — for most brands that's email (Klaviyo), reviews (Judge.me), and turning your video into sales (Quinn). Add subscriptions, upsells, and loyalty as you scale. And keep an eye on speed: prefer apps that are Built for Shopify and remove anything you're not using.

Questions

What Shopify apps does every store need?

At a minimum: email/SMS marketing, product reviews, and a way to convert traffic into sales (shoppable video and/or upsells). The list above covers those core categories with one strong pick each.

Do too many apps slow down a Shopify store?

They can. Favour apps that are Built for Shopify and lightweight — for example Quinn compresses video up to 20× and lazy-loads it, so shoppable video doesn't hurt page speed — and remove any app you're not actively using.

Are these apps free?

Most have a free plan or free trial. Several — Judge.me, Smile.io, Shopify Search & Discovery, and Quinn — offer genuinely free tiers to start.

What is the best shoppable video app for Shopify?

Quinn Shoppable Videos & Reels — Built for Shopify, a free plan, 20× compression so it stays fast, and a truly unlimited $49 plan (no view caps, unlike view-metered rivals).
