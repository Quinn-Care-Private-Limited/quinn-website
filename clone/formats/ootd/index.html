How to make an OOTD AI fashion video

An OOTD (“outfit of the day”) video shows one complete look, usually with the person walking or posing so the fit reads in motion. It’s the workhorse format of fashion social — simple, repeatable, and native to the feed.

Why the OOTD format works

It looks like a real person, not an ad

OOTD content blends into the feed instead of interrupting it, which is exactly what the platforms reward with reach.

One format, endless outfits

Because the structure is fixed, you can produce an OOTD for every SKU — the volume that performance testing needs.

Motion sells the fit

Walking and turning show drape, movement, and how a garment actually wears — things a still image can’t.
