How to make a GRWM AI fashion video

A GRWM (“get ready with me”) video follows an outfit coming together piece by piece, ending on the finished look. It’s one of the most-watched fashion formats because viewers stay to see how the look resolves.

Why the GRWM format works

The reveal keeps them watching

The whole video is a build-up to the final fit, so watch-time and completion rate run high — the signals the algorithm rewards.

It naturally stacks products

Getting ready shows several garments and accessories in one video, so a single GRWM can feature a whole outfit’s worth of SKUs.

The “becoming” arc is inherently engaging

Transformation content is a proven hook — people are wired to watch a before turn into an after.
