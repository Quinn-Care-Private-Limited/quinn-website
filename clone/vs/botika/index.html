What Clone is

Clone is an AI ad tool for fashion brands, built by Quinn. You paste a short-form fashion video that already went viral, upload photos of your garments, and Clone rebuilds that video — shot for shot — with your products on an AI model. The idea: a reel that already went viral has already passed the only test that matters, so you start from something that worked instead of guessing at new creative.

Most AI ad tools describe a reference video as a text prompt and regenerate it, which loses the rhythm and cuts that made it work. Clone goes directly video-to-video — it keeps the original structure and maps your exact garments onto it, fashion-native.
