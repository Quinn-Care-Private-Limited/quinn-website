Questions

What's the best AI ad tool for fashion brands?

It depends on the job — but for rebuilding a proven viral fashion reel with your own garments, Clone is the purpose-built, fashion-native choice.

Use Arcads for talking-head spokesperson ads, Creatify for a broad ecommerce ad suite, Higgsfield for directing original cinematic video, and Icon for done-for-you real human UGC.

What's the cheapest way to make fashion video ads with AI?

Clone prices by reference length: a 10-second 720p video is $9 on the starter pack, tapering to ~$4.30 at volume (packs from ~$18) — no subscription, and credits never expire.

Arcads is ~$11/video but locked behind $110/mo; Icon starts at $399/mo for human UGC; Creatify and Higgsfield are subscription/credit models from ~$39 and ~$15/mo.

How is Clone different from every other AI ad tool?

Every other tool makes you invent or produce the creative; Clone starts from a video that already went viral and rebuilds it with your products, so you inherit proven structure instead of guessing.

And it does it directly video-to-video — preserving the rhythm and cuts that made the original work — rather than describing and regenerating it.
