What Clone is

Clone copies a fashion video that already went viral — 1:1 — and swaps your products in. Built for performance marketers: instead of betting media budget on creative someone had to dream up, brief, shoot, and edit, you run a structure that already proved it performs. Early results back the bet — initial paying customers saw up to 343% higher ROAS compared with their in-house creatives, and 500+ videos have been cloned to date.

A 1:1 clone keeps the reference's cuts, camera motion, the main character's movement and their interaction with props, the music beats, effects, transitions, and background. Two things change: the person becomes an AI avatar you choose, and the clothes become yours, mapped from your garment photos. It works directly video-to-video — the reference is never flattened into a text prompt, which is why the things that made it perform survive. Most cloners are built for UGC talking heads; Clone is the first full 1:1 video-to-video cloner, and the first built for fashion.
