FAQ

What you're probably thinking.

Won't I get sued for cloning a competitor's ad?

You're not copying their products, their footage, or their brand — you're cloning the structure: the cuts, the pacing, the hook. The same way every brand on Instagram runs its own version of the same trend. The output has your products, your model, your brand. You're learning from what works, not stealing what's theirs.

Will it look like AI slop?

Clone doesn't generate from scratch — that's where slop comes from. It maps your products onto a proven, human-made structure, so the output inherits the polish of the reference. That's the whole trick.

Can it match my brand aesthetic?

You bring the products. You choose the model and pose. The reference dictates the vibe — and you chose the reference. You stay in control of every input.

What if my clone comes out wrong?

At $5, the worst-case loss is less than a coffee. And if a video genuinely doesn't clone as expected, flag it — we'll quietly make it right.

Who's behind this?

Clone is built by Quinn — the AI studio fashion brands already use for on-model product photography. Garment fidelity is our whole personality; Clone points it at video.
