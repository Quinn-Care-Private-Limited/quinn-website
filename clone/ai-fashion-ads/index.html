What Clone does, exactly

Clone takes two inputs: the link of a short-form fashion video that already performed (TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts) and photos of your garments. It rebuilds that video 1:1 — same structure, same energy — with the person replaced by an AI avatar you choose, wearing your clothes. The output is a 720p, 9:16 vertical ad, ready for Meta and TikTok, in about 15 minutes.

It is not a text-to-video generator, not an avatar reading a script, and not a slideshow maker. Nothing is invented. The creative decisions that made the reference perform were already made — by the creator who shot it and the audience that made it viral. Clone preserves them.

What "1:1" actually means

A clone keeps, from the reference video:

  • Every cut, in the same order, at the same timestamps
  • The camera motion — pans, pushes, whips, handheld shake
  • The main character's movement and how they interact with props
  • The music beats and how the edit lands on them
  • The effects and transitions
  • The background and setting

Exactly two things change: the person (replaced by an AI avatar of your choice) and the clothes (replaced by your garments, mapped from your photos). Proven video in, proven video out — wearing your brand.

Why this beats making ads from scratch

An in-house fashion ad means a concept, a model, samples, a shoot, an edit — days of work and $50–$200+ per finished video, all spent on a structure no audience has validated. And the hardest part isn't even the production; it's coming up with the idea. Clone removes both: the idea is the reel you picked, and the production is ~15 minutes of compute.

Most AI ad tools describe your reference in a text prompt and regenerate it — the rhythm and cuts get lost in translation. Clone goes directly video-to-video, so they survive.

What Clone can't do (yet)

Honesty over hype: Clone struggles with videos that have multiple main characters, split-screen edits, and extremely fast-paced cuts. If a clone comes out wrong, flag it — fixable ones are redone free, and anything Clone genuinely can't handle is refunded. A failed video never costs you money.
