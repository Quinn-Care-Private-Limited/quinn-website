Building a shoppable Instagram feed

A shoppable Instagram feed is your Instagram content, displayed on your own site, with the products in each post tagged so visitors can buy on the spot. It solves the oldest problem with social content: it makes people want things, then sends them somewhere they cannot buy.

Free plan · $9–$49/mo flat · Unlimited at $49 · ~5 min setup

What makes a feed "shoppable"

An ordinary embedded feed shows your posts. A shoppable feed adds two things: product tagging (each post is linked to the products in it) and an in-place buying path (tapping opens a product card with add-to-cart, without leaving the page). A third thing usually comes with it — attribution, so you can see which posts produced orders.

Why it beats a plain feed

  • It stops the leak. On a plain feed, the only action is a trip to Instagram. You lose the visitor at the exact moment they were interested.
  • It converts intent immediately. The gap between "I want that" and "I can buy that" collapses from several steps to one tap.
  • It makes social content measurable. Instead of engagement metrics, you get orders per post — which tells you what to make more of.
  • It reuses what you already made. No new content required; the reels exist already.

What it costs

Less than most people expect, because the content is already made. Free tiers exist; paid plans in this category typically run $9–$49/mo. Watch the metering: many tools charge by views or clicks, so the bill grows exactly when the feed works. Quinn is flat — $9, $19 or $49/mo, with unlimited views, videos and pages on the $49 plan.

How to build one on Shopify

  1. Install Quinn from the Shopify App Store — free plan, no theme code.
  2. Import your reels by pasting links.
  3. Tag the products in each reel; multi-product posts can carry several tags.
  4. Choose a layout — grid, carousel, stories bar, floating or PDP block — and place it.
  5. Watch the attribution and keep the posts that sell.

Roughly five minutes. Videos are compressed up to 20× and lazy-loaded from a CDN, so the feed does not slow the store down.

Questions

What is a shoppable Instagram feed?

It is your Instagram content displayed on your own website with the products in each post tagged, so a visitor can tap a post and add the product to cart without leaving. It usually also reports orders per post, which turns social content into a measurable channel.

How do I make my Instagram feed shoppable on Shopify?

Install an app that supports product tagging, import your reels, tag the products in each, and place the feed as a grid, carousel or stories bar. With Quinn this takes about five minutes and needs no theme code, and there is a free plan to try it.

Is a shoppable Instagram feed worth it?

It is worth it if you already post product content, because it converts attention you have already earned rather than requiring new content. The measurable version of the answer comes from per-post attribution: if posts are producing add-to-carts and orders, it is paying for itself.

Do I need Instagram Shopping to have a shoppable feed?

No. Instagram Shopping tags products inside Instagram; a shoppable feed tags them on your own site. They are independent — you can run a shoppable feed on your store whether or not Instagram Shopping is set up.
