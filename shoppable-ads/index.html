Shoppable ads, end to end

A shoppable ad lets someone buy from the ad unit itself, or lands them somewhere they can buy in one more tap. The format matters less than the thing most advertisers get wrong: paying for a click that lands on a page which restarts the whole persuasion job from scratch.

Free plan · $9–$49/mo flat · Unlimited at $49 · ~5 min setup

What counts as a shoppable ad

  • In-app checkout ads — the purchase completes inside the platform (TikTok Shop, Instagram Shopping where available).
  • Product-tagged ads — tapping a tagged product opens a product view, then your store.
  • Catalogue and collection ads — a video or image plus a product carousel drawn from your feed.
  • Shoppable landing experiences — a normal ad pointing at a page where video and imagery are themselves shoppable.

The mistake that wastes the budget

Most shoppable-ad advice stops at the ad. But the ad is not where the money leaks — the landing page is. You pay for a click from someone who just watched fifteen seconds of engaging video, and you drop them onto a static product page that starts the persuasion over with a photo and a paragraph. The momentum is gone.

The fix is continuity: land them on the same video they clicked, playing, with the product tagged and buyable underneath. The shopper picks up exactly where the ad left off. That is a landing-page change rather than an ad-platform change, and it is usually the cheapest improvement available to a paid-social account.

Creative that works

  • Hook in the first second. There is no second chance in feed.
  • Vertical, sound-off legible. Assume mute; caption anything essential.
  • Show the product doing its job, not a logo animation.
  • One product per ad. Catalogue carousels handle breadth; the video should sell one thing.
  • Test volume beats polish. Creative is the biggest lever in paid social, and you find winners by running many, not by perfecting one.

Questions

What is a shoppable ad?

A shoppable ad lets someone buy from the ad itself or reach a purchase in one more tap — through in-app checkout, tagged products, or a catalogue attached to the creative. The goal is removing steps between seeing the product and buying it.

Which platforms support shoppable ads?

Meta offers collection and catalogue ads with product tagging, TikTok offers video shopping ads and in-app checkout through TikTok Shop in supported markets, Pinterest has Product Pins and shopping ads, and YouTube can attach product feeds to video campaigns.

Do shoppable ads perform better than regular ads?

They tend to perform better when they genuinely remove steps, because every step between intent and purchase loses people. The bigger gain for most advertisers is fixing the landing experience — sending paid traffic to a page where the video keeps playing and the product is buyable, rather than a static product page.

What do I need to run shoppable ads?

A clean, current product catalogue or feed connected to the platform, creative shot vertically and legible with sound off, and a landing experience that continues the ad rather than restarting the pitch.
