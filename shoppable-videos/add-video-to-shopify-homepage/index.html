Add video to your Shopify homepage

The homepage is the most-loaded page on your store, which makes it the best place for video and the worst place to get video wrong. A hero video that takes four seconds to appear costs you visitors before they see anything. Here is how to add it properly.

Free plan · $9–$49/mo flat · Unlimited at $49 · ~5 min setup

Method 1 — A theme video section (hero banner)

  1. Online Store → Themes → Customize.
  2. Add section and choose the video or video banner block your theme offers.
  3. Upload an MP4 or paste a YouTube/Vimeo URL, then set the overlay text and heading.
  4. Save and preview on mobile before you publish.

Best for one big brand moment at the top. Watch out: a hero video often becomes the Largest Contentful Paint element, so an uncompressed file directly damages your Core Web Vitals.

Method 2 — A video carousel or stories bar (an app)

Instead of one hero video, a row of shoppable clips — a carousel of styled videos, or a circular stories bar like Instagram's. This tends to outperform a hero video on stores that sell many products, because it invites browsing rather than just setting a mood, and every clip is tappable.

Method 3 — A floating video widget

A small player that follows the shopper as they browse. It costs no layout space and keeps working on pages where you would never place a video block.

The five speed rules

  1. Compress before upload. A hero video should be measured in low single-digit megabytes, not tens.
  2. Never autoplay with sound. Muted autoplay only — browsers block the rest anyway, and visitors hate it.
  3. Use a poster image. Something meaningful paints immediately while video loads.
  4. Lazy-load everything below the fold. Do not pay for video nobody scrolls to.
  5. Avoid YouTube embeds at the top. The third-party script cost lands exactly where it hurts most.

Then check the result: run PageSpeed Insights before and after, and look at Online Store → Themes → Store speed.

Questions

How do I add a video to my Shopify homepage?

Go to Online Store, Themes, Customize, add a section, and choose your theme's video or video banner block, then upload an MP4 or paste a YouTube/Vimeo URL. For a row of shoppable clips or a stories bar rather than a single hero video, use an app.

Should I autoplay video on my Shopify homepage?

Muted autoplay is fine and usual for hero video; autoplay with sound is not — browsers block it and visitors dislike it. Always set a poster image so something meaningful renders while the video loads.

Does a homepage video hurt page speed?

It can, badly, because a hero video often becomes the Largest Contentful Paint element. Compress before uploading, use a poster image, lazy-load anything below the fold, and avoid YouTube embeds near the top of the page.

What is the best video format for a Shopify homepage?

MP4 with H.264 is the most reliable across browsers and devices. Use 16:9 for a hero banner and 9:16 vertical for social-style carousels and stories bars.

Is a video carousel better than a hero video?

For stores with many products, usually yes — a carousel of shoppable clips invites browsing and each clip is tappable, whereas a hero video sets a mood without offering a next step. Stores with one flagship product often do better with the hero.
