Turn your Instagram Reels into shoppable Shopify content.

Your Reels already earn the scroll on Instagram. Quinn brings them onto your Shopify store — tappable, tagged with products, and checkout-ready — by importing straight from a reel link. No re-uploading, no re-editing, no developer.

Why put Instagram Reels on your store

A reel that performs on Instagram is proof a product resonates — but that momentum usually dies at the link in bio. Embedding the same reel on your storefront lets a shopper watch it and buy in the same tap, instead of bouncing between apps. It also gives your best social content a second life on the pages where purchase intent is highest: the homepage, the product page, and the cart.

Import a reel in one click

  1. Paste the Instagram Reel link into Quinn — the video imports automatically.
  2. Tag the products featured in the reel from your Shopify catalog.
  3. Drop it onto any page as a carousel, story, floating bubble, or grid, and publish.

Because Quinn works from the link, you never re-download or re-encode anything. The reel is compressed up to 20× with AI and served from a CDN, so it looks sharp without dragging your page speed down.

Where your reels can show up

  • Stories row at the top of your homepage or collection — the Instagram pattern shoppers already know.
  • Carousel of reels on the homepage or product page.
  • Floating bubble that follows the shopper and expands into a shoppable reel.
  • Grid — a full shoppable feed on a dedicated “shop the reels” page.

See which reels actually sell

Every view, tap, add-to-cart and order is tracked end-to-end, with page- and media-level revenue attribution — so you know which reels drive sales, not just plays, and it syncs with Meta Pixel and Google Analytics.
