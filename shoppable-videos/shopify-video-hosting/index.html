Shopify video hosting, compared

Where your video is hosted decides how fast it loads, whether competitors' videos appear next to yours, and how much control you have over quality. There are four realistic options for a Shopify store, and the right answer depends on whether the video is decoration or is supposed to sell something.

Free plan · $9–$49/mo flat · Unlimited at $49 · ~5 min setup

Option 1 — Shopify Files / native media

Upload directly to Shopify. It is hosted on Shopify's CDN, and there is no extra cost or third-party script.

Good: simple, fast, no branding from anyone else. Limits: file size caps, limited control over encoding, and it can only appear where Shopify and your theme allow — with no tagging or attribution.

Option 2 — YouTube

Host on YouTube and embed. Free, unlimited, and the video also lives on the world's second-largest search engine.

Good: free, great reach, adaptive streaming. Limits: a heavy third-party player script, YouTube branding, and — the real problem — related videos and end screens can surface competitors inside your own store. Fine for a blog post; risky on a product page.

Option 3 — Vimeo

Paid hosting with clean, brandable players and no competitor recommendations.

Good: professional player, privacy controls, no third-party suggestions. Limits: another subscription, still a third-party embed, and no product tagging or commerce attribution.

Option 4 — A shoppable video app

The app hosts, compresses and serves the video, and adds what none of the above do: product tagging, in-video add-to-cart, and revenue attribution per video.

Good: built for commerce, optimised delivery, no theme code. Limits: a subscription, and unnecessary if the video is purely decorative.

The recommendation

  • A single decorative clip? Shopify Files. No reason to add anything.
  • Long-form content or a blog? YouTube — the reach is worth the script, and the risk of competitor suggestions is low off the product page.
  • Brand film where polish matters? Vimeo.
  • Video that should sell? A shoppable app — because hosting is the easy part, and tagging plus attribution is the part that makes video pay for itself.

Questions

Where should I host video for my Shopify store?

For a single decorative clip, Shopify's native media is simplest. For long-form or blog content, YouTube's reach is worth it. For brand films, Vimeo. For video meant to drive sales, use a shoppable video app so the video can be tagged, bought from, and attributed to revenue.

Is it bad to embed YouTube videos on Shopify?

It has two real costs: a heavy third-party player script, and the possibility that related videos or end screens surface competitors inside your own store. That is acceptable on a blog post and risky on a product page.

Does Shopify host video for free?

Yes — you can upload video to product media and Shopify Files at no extra cost, served from Shopify's CDN. There are file-size limits, limited encoding control, and no product tagging or revenue attribution.

What is the best video format for Shopify?

MP4 using H.264 is the most compatible choice across browsers and devices. Keep files compressed, use 9:16 for social-style widgets and 16:9 for hero banners.
