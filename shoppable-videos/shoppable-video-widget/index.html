A shoppable video widget lets shoppers watch and buy in the same place. Quinn gives you every format — carousel, stories, floating bubble, grid, and pop-up — from one library, each tappable, trackable, and matched to your theme.

What a shoppable video widget is

It is an embeddable block of video that carries product tags: a shopper taps a product inside the video and adds it to cart or checks out without leaving the widget. That collapses the usual watch-then-hunt-for-the-product journey into a single tap.

Every widget type, one library

  • Carousel — a swipeable row of videos on any page.
  • Stories — tappable circles that open full-screen.
  • Floating bubble — a video that follows the shopper.
  • Grid — a full shoppable video feed.
  • Pop-up — a timed or triggered shoppable video.

Add one to any page in 5 minutes

  1. Upload videos or import reels and TikToks by link.
  2. Tag products and style the widget in the visual editor.
  3. Place it on any page and publish — no theme code.

Fast by default

Every widget is powered by video compressed up to 20× with AI, CDN-served and lazy-loaded, so rich video never costs you page speed.

Built-in analytics

Track views, taps, add-to-carts, and orders per widget, with page- and media-level revenue attribution.
